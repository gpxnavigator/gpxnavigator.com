Built for the road, not the desk.

One job: getting you past the points on your route. Anything that doesn't help with that isn't in here.

Every GPX, unchanged

Share your file from mail, WhatsApp, Files, or Drive. The points stay in the order they appear in the file — the app never reorders or recalculates them.

Point by point

Distance and heading to the next waypoint, in numbers you can read at speed with gloves on. Reached or skipped: the app moves on.

For every way of travelling

Choose motorcycle, car, bike, or walking. The app adjusts the arrival threshold, the map zoom, and the units — same route, different measure.

Offline on the road

Routes live on your device. Without signal, points, distances, and coordinates keep working — only the map tiles wait for a connection.

Eleven languages

The app follows your device's language, or you pick one yourself. From Dutch and German to Czech, Swedish, and Portuguese.

Your route stays yours

No account, no cloud, no ride history on a server. Your location never leaves your device and is never used for ads — see the privacy policy.

From file to ride in three steps.

1

Share the GPX with the app

Open the file from your mail or chat and pick GPX Navigator from the share sheet. The route lands in your library right away, with a name, point count, and total distance.

2

Pick your mode of travel

Motorcycle, car, bike, or walking. Check the waypoints, rename the route to something you'll recognise, and confirm the first points look right.

3

Start and hit the road

The app asks for location access when you start and switches to drive mode: big numbers, dark screen, two buttons. Stop halfway, and it picks up right where you left off.

Who it's for

Touring rides, day trips, and inspection rounds.

  • Motorcycle rides and car routes you plan yourself or with a club
  • Cycling tours and node-network routes from a tourist board's GPX
  • Hikes along fixed points, even without signal in the woods
  • Inspection and service rounds along fixed locations
Not for: address-based turn-by-turn navigation. Your existing maps app remains better suited for that.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app follow the route, or just the points?

Just the points, in the order they appear in the file. You'll see the line between points on the map, but the app doesn't steer you along a recalculated road — you choose how you get to the next point.

When do I see an ad?

Only at a natural resting point: after you finish a route, or between two screens. Never during navigation, never over the map, and never on CarPlay or Android Auto. For $2.99 you turn them off once, for good.

Does the app work for cycling and walking too?

Yes. You pick your mode of travel per route; the app adjusts the threshold at which a point counts as reached and the map zoom accordingly. On foot that threshold is small; on the motorcycle it's more generous.

Do I need a signal?

No. Points, distances, heading, and coordinates come from your device and GPS. Only the map image needs data; without signal you keep navigating on the point list and the direction arrow.

Where does my ride data stay?

On your phone. There's no account and no server that routes or locations go to. See the privacy policy for the full explanation.
